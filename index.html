shield 5. Risk Mitigation

Stopping Hallucinations

In enterprise contexts, a wrong answer is worse than no answer. Two mandatory strategies:

  • check_circle
    Mandatory Context Adherence "Answer ONLY using the information in the provided tags."
  • check_circle
    Controlled Uncertainty "If the answer is not in the text, respond exactly with 'I don't know'."
Without Strategy
"The projected revenue is $5M."
(Hallucinated)
With Strategy
"I don't know."
(Safe)
